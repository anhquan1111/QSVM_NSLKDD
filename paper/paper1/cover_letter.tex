\textbf{Subject: Revised manuscript TETC-2026-05-0252, ``NISQ-Aware Quantum
Kernel SVM for Network Intrusion Detection: A Regime-Specific Benchmark on
NSL-KDD''}

Dear Dr.\ Schaumont,

Please find enclosed the revised version of the manuscript above, submitted in
response to the decision of 14 August 2026 (major revision). We are grateful to
the Associate Editor and the four reviewers; their comments led us to re-run
every experiment under a corrected protocol, and the revised paper reports a
narrower and partly negative result than the one we submitted.

\textbf{Files enclosed.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item The clean revised manuscript, without annotations or highlighting
    (\texttt{main\_revision.pdf}).
  \item An annotated copy in which new or changed text, and every added or
    corrected bibliography entry, is set on a yellow background, and every
    subsection heading carries a tag stating what changed and which reviewer
    item prompted it (\texttt{main\_annotated.pdf}).
  \item A response letter addressing each comment of the Associate Editor and
    of Reviewers 1 to 4 individually, with a summary of differences, a section
    on corrections we identified ourselves, and a dedicated section for the
    Editor-in-Chief and the Associate Editor itemising every change to the
    bibliography with its reason (\texttt{response\_letter.pdf}).
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Page length and Mandatory Overlength Page Charges.} The original
submission was 11 pages. The revised manuscript, which now includes the
biographies of all five authors, the second dataset, the two additional
baselines and the sample-complexity sweep the reviewers requested, is 17 pages
in the IEEE two-column format. As requested in the decision letter, we state
the following: ALL submissions exceeding 12 pages at any time of the review
process should explicitly report, in the related cover letter, a declaration
that the authors are aware of the MOPC policies and, when due, will pay the
related charges without requesting any future waiving. Please note that the
MOPC policies and the amount of final charges do effectively apply to ALL
submissions exceeding 12 pages after final layout of the accepted manuscript.
We are aware of the MOPC policies and, when due, will pay the related charges
without requesting any future waiving.

\textbf{Bibliography.} The revised bibliography has 38 entries (the submitted
version had 36): six entries were added, four removed and one corrected. No
self-citation was added or removed; the manuscript contains none. Each change
and its reason is listed in the section ``Changes to the bibliography'' of the
response letter, and the added and corrected entries are highlighted in the
annotated copy.

\textbf{Authors, affiliations, acknowledgments.} The author list and the order
of authors are unchanged. No co-author has changed affiliation since the
original submission. The funding acknowledgment (The University of Danang -
University of Science and Technology, Project T2026-02-10TN) is unchanged.
Author biographies, which the submitted version carried, are included in the
revised manuscript.

\textbf{Reproducibility.} The complete implementation, the frozen protocol
files, every intermediate artifact, the figure-generation scripts and four audit
scripts are released at \url{https://github.com/anhquan1111/QSVM_NSLKDD}; the
commit hash is stated in Sec.~IV-D of the manuscript.

The manuscript has not been published previously and is not under
consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the revised version.

Thank you for your consideration.
